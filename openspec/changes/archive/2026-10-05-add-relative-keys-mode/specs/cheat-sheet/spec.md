# Spec Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Cheat sheet content
The cheat sheet SHALL show the whole/half-step patterns for the major (2 2 1 2 2 2 1), natural minor (2 1 2 2 1 2 2) and harmonic minor (2 1 2 2 1 3 1) scales, the diatonic triad qualities for major and minor keys, the scale-degree names, and the rule for relative keys: the relative minor starts on the 6th note of a major scale and the relative major starts on the 3rd note of a minor scale.

#### Scenario: Reference content
- **WHEN** the cheat sheet is open
- **THEN** the major-key triad pattern major, minor, minor, major, major, minor, diminished is shown

#### Scenario: Relative-key rule
- **WHEN** the cheat sheet is open
- **THEN** it states that the relative minor starts on the 6th note of the major scale and the relative major on the 3rd note of the minor scale
